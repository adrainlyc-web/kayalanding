About

This site is an independent guide to the 918Kaya app for players in Papua New Guinea. It explains what the app is, how to download it, how game IDs work and how payments in kina usually work.

What we are not

We are not the 918Kaya developer and we don't issue 918Kaya game IDs. We are not affiliated with 918Kaya.

How we earn

The Register and Login buttons open Kundu365 through a referral link. If you sign up there, we may earn a commission. It doesn't change what you pay. Kundu365 is a different platform from 918Kaya, with its own account and rules.

Our approach

We keep the facts simple and say where we haven't verified something. If you spot an error, please tell us so we can correct it.
