Responsible Gaming

Gambling should be entertainment, not a way to make money or fix money problems.

18+ only

You must be 18 or older. Don't let anyone under 18 use your phone, account or game ID.

Set a budget

  • Only play with money you can afford to lose.
  • Never use money for food, school fees, rent or bills.
  • Decide a limit before you deposit and stick to it.
  • Don't chase losses and never borrow to gamble.

Warning signs

  • Spending more time or money than planned
  • Gambling to escape stress or sadness
  • Lying to family about how much you play
  • Borrowing money or missing bills to keep playing
  • Feeling unable to stop

How to take a break

  • Stop depositing.
  • Delete the 918Kaya app and mute or block agent chats.
  • Take a set break, even just a week.
  • Talk to a family member, church leader or counsellor you trust.

Getting help

If gambling is causing problems for you or someone close to you, please talk to someone you trust, such as family, a community leader, or a health worker or counsellor. You don't have to handle it alone.
